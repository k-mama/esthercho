# Cinematic Homepage Hero

## Purpose

The homepage opens with a full viewport cinematic sequence.

The video reaches every edge of the viewport. The shared site header is placed over the moving image only on the homepage.

## Visual Direction

- Full bleed video
- Transparent navy gradient behind the navigation
- Gold Esther Cho wordmark
- Bright warm ivory navigation labels
- Centered lower hero copy
- No white header card on the homepage
- Existing paper room sections continue below the first viewport

## Video Behavior

The background film is muted, loops, and uses `playsInline`.

A small pause and play control is provided.

Visitors who prefer reduced motion see the poster frame instead of the moving video.

## Provenance Boundary

The supplied video is a cinematic opening asset.

It must not be described as documentary footage, a childhood photograph, family archive material, or verified footage of the actual childhood house.

Real photographs used elsewhere on the site remain separately identified.
