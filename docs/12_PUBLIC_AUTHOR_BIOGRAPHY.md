# Esther Cho Public Author Biography

## Public Biography Draft

Esther Cho is a Korean writer and artist whose work grows from Christian faith, memory, childhood, and the quiet textures of ordinary life.

She studied at Yewon School and Seoul Arts High School before earning a degree in Oriental Painting from Seoul National University. She later completed the coursework for a master's program in Art Education at Ewha Womans University.

Her creative world brings together writing, hand-drawn art, family memory, devotional reflection, and stories shaped by lived experience. Much of that creative life has grown within the ordinary rhythms of home, family, faith, and service.

She is a writer, artist, and homemaker. She also supports North Korean refugees through mentoring and participates in mission work in Bangladesh.

## Approved Educational Record

- Yewon School, graduated in 1985
- Seoul Arts High School, graduated in 1988
- Seoul National University, College of Fine Arts, Department of Oriental Painting, graduated in 1992
- Ewha Womans University, Art Education master's program coursework completed in 1999
