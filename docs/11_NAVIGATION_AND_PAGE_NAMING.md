# Esther Cho Navigation and Page Naming

## Purpose

The Esther Cho website uses short, understandable navigation labels together with more literary page titles.

The header must remain easy to scan. The page title may carry the deeper emotional and literary identity of each section.

## Approved Navigation

| Header label | Route | Signature page title |
|---|---|---|
| STORIES | `/stories/` | Stories Born from Life |
| BOOKS | `/books/` | Beyond the Garden |
| CHILDHOOD | `/archive/` | The Childhood Album |
| STUDIO | `/studio/` | Drawn by Hand, Reborn in Light |
| MORNING TABLE | `/notes/` | The Morning Table |
| ESTHER | `/about/` | The House That Became a Story |

## Naming Principles

- Header labels must remain short enough for practical navigation.
- Signature titles may be poetic and editorial.
- URLs remain stable and simple.
- The website wordmark continues to serve as the home link.
- A separate HOME menu item must not be restored.
- The site should feel like entering a house of stories rather than browsing a catalogue.

## Route Stability

The naming change does not alter any existing route.

`CHILDHOOD` continues to use `/archive/`.

`MORNING TABLE` continues to use `/notes/`.

`ESTHER` continues to use `/about/`.
