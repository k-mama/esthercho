# Esther Cho Website · Editorial Review of 18 Stories

Review date: November 21, 2026  
Scope: Eighteen Korean/English life stories and their reading experience.

## Source hierarchy
1. `PART I, II, III.docx`: the author's direct spoken reflections, used to verify tone, personal conviction, and cadence.
2. `내가 좋아하는 것들과 내가 사랑하는 삶.docx`: longer edited collection used to cross-check scenes, people, and objects.
3. `조성연 작가의 어린 시절과 기억.docx`: edited childhood chronology used for relationships, places, and chronology.
4. The in-laws' meals and 57 authentic food photos were directly confirmed by the publisher. The period remains *approximately six or seven years*.

## Editorial decisions
- Prefer the author's concrete memories, tastes, and dry humor over the editor's explanatory endings.
- Do not introduce psychological motivations, dates, or dialogue not supported by the sources.
- Remove mechanical symmetry or ornate generalities in North American English without rewriting Esther's temperament.
- Move repeated provenance disclaimers out of public-facing articles, preserving them in the internal source register.
- Replace generic next-story labels with the actual next article title.
