## Editorial Boundaries

The following uploaded collections are preserved for later review and are not used in this first ESTHER page implementation:

- funeral photographs
- wedding photographs
- photographs of the author's child
- extended family photographs
- photographs of parents
- large meal archives
- additional refugee camp photographs showing identifiable children
- AI reimagined house or table images

These materials may be used later only with a clear page purpose, appropriate captions, and a privacy review.

## Accuracy Rules

- Do not describe the funeral image as a general family celebration.
- Do not identify third parties unless their identity and publication permission are confirmed.
- Do not label AI assisted imagery as a childhood photograph, family record, or historical archive.
- Do not state that Esther Cho earned a master's degree.
- Do not invent the name of the North Korean refugee organization.
