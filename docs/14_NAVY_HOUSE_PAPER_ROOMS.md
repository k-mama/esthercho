# Navy House, Paper Rooms

## Direction

The Esther Cho website uses a deep navy outer field and warm paper rooms.

The navy field represents depth, night, faith, and the quiet exterior of the house.

The paper rooms hold memory, photographs, books, the morning table, and hand drawn work.

## Interface Principles

- The website must not resemble a SaaS dashboard.
- A paper room is a large editorial section, not a small repetitive UI card.
- Real photographs remain the emotional center of the page.
- Border radii stay restrained.
- Shadows are avoided.
- Gold appears only in small labels, fine rules, and brand details.
- Navy space is allowed to remain visible between rooms.
- The sequence should feel like moving through a house rather than scanning a catalogue.

## Header and Footer

The header and footer remain warm paper panels so the transparent navy wordmark stays legible.

## Homepage Rooms

1. Welcome to Esther's House
2. The House That Became a Story
3. The Morning Table
4. The Childhood Album
5. Drawn by Hand, Reborn in Light

## Provenance

The homepage uses real photographs and an original hand drawn work supplied by Esther Cho.

AI assisted images must not be presented as childhood photographs, family records, or historical archive material.
