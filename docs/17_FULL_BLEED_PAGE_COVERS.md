## Rules

- The homepage remains the only moving cover.
- Every subpage opens with one still photograph or original artwork.
- The image reaches all viewport edges.
- The shared navigation sits over the image in warm ivory.
- The transparent wordmark is rendered in restrained gold.
- The first viewport is visual. Reading begins on the paper section below.
- No AI-generated image is presented as personal archive material.
- Original film texture and age are preserved.
