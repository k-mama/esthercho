# Esther Cho Website — Project Brief

## 1. Project Identity

**Public author brand:** Esther Cho  
**Korean real name:** 조성연 / Cho Seongyeon  
**Website type:** Global author website, living archive, book showcase, visual storytelling home  
**Default audience:** North American English readers  
**Secondary audience:** Korean readers  
**Expansion audience:** Readers in additional languages

The website is not a conventional author portfolio, e-commerce catalog, or memoir archive. It is an invitation into a real house of stories where childhood memory, true-life storytelling, hand-drawn books, morning devotion, photographs, and reimagined visual worlds meet.

## 2. Core Experience

The opening experience should make visitors feel that they have been invited into Esther Cho’s home.

The intended emotional sequence is:

1. See the childhood home and garden
2. Watch the gate open
3. Move gently toward the house
4. Enter Esther Cho’s world
5. Discover that the real house became part of a storybook
6. Encounter the morning devotional table and the book *Manna of Table*
7. Move through childhood photographs, family memory, books, drawings, and films

The experience must feel warm, refined, intimate, slow, and welcoming.

It must not feel like:
- a luxury real-estate website
- a Christian stock-photo website
- a corporate publishing company
- a museum for a deceased person
- a children’s entertainment portal
- an AI image showcase

## 3. Brand Essence

- Invitation
- Memory
- True Story
- Morning Devotion
- Hand-Drawn Wonder

## 4. Primary Content Pillars

### The Real House
The real childhood home, garden, gate, rooms, family spaces, and the home as it appears in stories.

### The Morning Table
Early-morning devotion, the devotional table, breakfast after prayer, and *Manna of Table*.

### The Childhood Archive
Family photographs, childhood images, places, objects, and remembered scenes.

### The Reimagined Storybook
Original hand-drawn artwork, real-life source material, AI-assisted reimagining, and the final published world.

### Books
Devotional books and children’s books, with clear separation but shared authorship.

### Notes and Films
New memories, short essays, voice notes, living photographs, memory films, and book films.

## 5. Public Name Strategy

Use **ESTHER CHO** as the primary global wordmark and author identity.

Disclose the Korean real name clearly in:
- About
- Korean-language pages
- Book metadata
- Press kit
- Rights information
- Official biography

Recommended English disclosure:

> Esther Cho is the pen name of Korean author Cho Seongyeon.

Recommended Korean disclosure:

> 조성연은 Esther Cho라는 필명으로 묵상집과 동화, 삶의 기억을 쓰는 작가입니다.

Do not display `ESTHER CHO / CHO SEONGYEON / 조성연` as three competing logos in the hero.

## 6. Editorial Position

The English website is the editorial master.

English copy must be written naturally for North American readers. Korean text is not to be translated line by line into English.

The voice should be:
- intimate
- precise
- quietly literary
- emotionally clear
- warm without sentimentality
- faith-rooted without preaching
- premium without luxury-brand affectation

## 7. Product Goal

The first release must establish a scalable system for stories, books, archive images, places, people, audio, films, language versions, and future editorial updates.

The site should be able to grow from a small launch collection into a large living archive without redesigning the entire system.
